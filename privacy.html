Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version

Everything you put into perCent stays on your iPhone. perCent has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and it never connects to your bank. We don't collect, see, sell or share any of your data.

What perCent stores, and where

  • Statements you import: the transactions in CSV files you choose to open in perCent (date, merchant, amount), the file's name so you can find or remove an import later, plus the cards, categories and repeating expenses you set up.
  • Your settings: things like your income, province, budget targets, car costs and credit card preferences.

All of this is stored only on your device. It is excluded from iCloud and computer backups, and if the app's data is ever restored or transferred to a different device, perCent erases it rather than carrying it over.

Backups you save: if you use Save a backup, perCent writes your data to a file in the place you choose (for example Files or iCloud Drive). That file is under your control, isn't encrypted by perCent, and is only read back if you choose Restore from a backup.

What perCent does not do

  • It doesn't send your data anywhere. The app makes no network requests for your data.
  • It doesn't ask for bank logins or connect to financial institutions.
  • It has no advertising, tracking, analytics or third-party SDKs.
  • It doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Credit card information

Card details, fees and offers in perCent are built into the app from issuers' own public websites. Card data is updated as of November 23, 2026; terms and offers change often, so confirm them with the issuer before you apply. Point values are perCent's own estimates. Looking at cards in perCent doesn't tell any bank anything about you. perCent isn't affiliated with any bank or card network and doesn't earn referral fees.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Data & privacy → Erase all data in the app, or delete the app. Either removes everything perCent stored.

Children

perCent isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact
