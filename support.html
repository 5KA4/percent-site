How do I add my spending?

Download a CSV statement from your bank's website (most Canadian banks offer one under statements or transaction history), then tap the import button on the Overview tab, or open the file in perCent from the Files app.

A purchase has the wrong category

Tap the transaction and pick the right category. perCent learns from your fixes and applies them to that merchant from then on. "Help perCent learn" on the Transactions tab lists merchants it's unsure about.

How are card recommendations made?

Card combos compares Canadian credit cards against your own spending, then adds the perks you said matter (lounges, insurance, no foreign transaction fees) and subtracts fees. It's an estimate for information only, not financial or credit advice. Terms and offers change often, so confirm them on the issuer's site before you apply.

Is my data safe?

Everything stays on your iPhone. perCent has no servers and never connects to your bank. See the privacy policy.

Moving to a new iPhone

perCent's data isn't in iCloud backups, so it doesn't move on its own. On your old iPhone, go to Settings → Data & privacy → Save a backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (iCloud Drive works). On the new iPhone, open perCent and choose Restore from a backup.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Data & privacy → Erase all data, or delete the app.
